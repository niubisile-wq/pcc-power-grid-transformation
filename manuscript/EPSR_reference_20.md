## What EPSR writing seems to do well

- Titles are specific, method-first, and application-bound.
- Abstracts usually need one sentence each for problem, method, system, benchmark, and quantitative gain.
- Results are often reported with exact system scale, scenario count, and effect size.
- Claims are usually operational, not philosophical: the paper says what was blocked, predicted, scheduled, or stabilized.
- The writing tends to separate method contribution from deployment consequence.
